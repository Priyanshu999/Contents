# Conclusion

## Key Achievements

**Technical Implementation**
- Successfully implemented 4 processing paths and integrated APV codec despite platform constraints
- Successfully handled format conversion challenges
- Successfully developed Android APK for proof-of-concept demonstration

**Performance Results**
- Achieved on average at least 25% bitrate reduction on images without loss of perceptual quality
- Demonstrated that preprocessing yields significant bitrate reduction for equal perceived quality

**Research & Development**
- Comprehensive survey of various preprocessing methodologies
- Initiated development of deep learning-based preprocessor following Chadha's paper
- Enhanced perceptual metrics calculation methodology

**System Optimization**
